Definitions and method

Ledger. Every share movement comes from ERC-1155 transfers. Each is explained by an exchange fill (exact price and fee), then by a migration, conversion, redeem, merge or split in the same transaction, else as a wallet transfer. Redeem cash is the collateral paid to the wallet in that transaction.

Verified positions. Wallet-token pairs whose opening position appeared in the first API snapshot. Pairs touched while a snapshot was being read are excluded.

Smart-money definitions. PnL skill: t-statistic of return on capital across events. Calibration: wins beyond what entry prices implied, as a z-score. Longshot buying: the same for entries under 25c. Conviction: capital-weighted edge per share, shrunk toward zero for wallets with few events. All use only trades resolved before the cutoff.

Insider signature. A young, low-activity account holding a large position in a market about to resolve. Monitoring only.

HHI. Sum of squared holder shares, 0 to 10,000, over the top holders the API returns (up to 400 per outcome).

Fee bps. Taker fees divided by taker notional, in basis points. Protocol rates are 0.04 to 0.07 x p(1-p) per share, so the cost is highest near 50c.
