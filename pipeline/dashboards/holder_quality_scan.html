Actionable takeaway

Watch-list rule: the side the smart wallets lean toward is priced under 50%, the lean is at least 75%, the market has traded at least $100k, and at least 3 smart wallets hold it. Use it to decide what to research, not what to copy.

    Tag definitions

    no-basis: 1,000+ shares at under $1 cost (transfers, contracts); ignored.

    MM/arb: holds both sides, or lifetime volume above $5M with absolute ROI under 0.5%.

    INSIDER-LIKE: pattern score 60+ of 100 on a ticket of at least $2,000 that is not a hedge. Points for a young wallet, few lifetime trades, this market being a large share of lifetime volume, a large ticket, and $3k+ bought at 30 c or less. 40–59 is watch-fresh.

    SMART: record score 70+ of 100 after a gate of 60 active days, 100 trades and $50k all-time PnL. Points for age, trades, PnL, ROI, Sharpe, winning days, positive 30-day windows, no single lucky day, and limited drawdown. 50–69 is solid. Above 20k fills marks a bot or quant, kept but counted at half weight in the lean.
